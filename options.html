Settings

Keyword rules, excluded domains, and session backup

Excluded domains

One pattern per line. These tabs are skipped during auto-grouping (e.g. chrome://, localhost).

Smart keyword rules

Keywords are matched against tab title and URL (case-insensitive). Used by Smart Group after domain grouping.

Session backup

Export all saved sessions as JSON, or import from a previous backup.

Keyboard shortcuts

Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts to customize:

  • Ctrl+Shift+O — Open popup
  • Ctrl+Shift+G — Auto group by domain
  • Ctrl+Shift+, — Collapse all groups
